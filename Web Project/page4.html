When did we start? 
We began our sweet journey in 2026, with a simple dream: to turn cakes into moments of joy, love, and celebration.
From the very first recipe, every layer has carried a story, and every flavor has been a promise of happiness.
where can you find us? 
You can find us in the heart of Egypt, where every cake is baked with love and a touch of sweetness.
 Our store is more than a place — it's a cozy corner where joy is served fresh, one slice at a time.
